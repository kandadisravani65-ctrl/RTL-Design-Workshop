# Synthesis Results

## Introduction

After synthesis, the generated reports can be analyzed to understand the characteristics of the design.

---

# Important Parameters

## 1. Cell Count

The total number of standard cells used by the synthesized design.

---

## 2. Sequential Cells

Sequential cells include storage elements such as flip-flops.

---

## 3. Combinational Cells

Combinational cells include logic gates and other combinational functions.

---

## 4. Area

Area represents the estimated physical area occupied by the synthesized cells.

---

## 5. Timing

Timing information indicates whether the design can meet the required timing constraints.

---

## 6. Net Count

Net count represents the number of electrical connections between cells.

---

# Why Analyze Synthesis Results?

Synthesis analysis helps evaluate:

- Design complexity
- Area
- Timing
- Standard-cell usage
- Optimization requirements

---

## Key Learning

Synthesis reports provide important information about the design before moving to physical implementation.
