# Floorplanning

## What is Floorplanning?

Floorplanning is the process of deciding the physical organization of a chip before detailed placement and routing.

---

# Floorplan Defines

- Die area
- Core area
- Aspect ratio
- Standard-cell rows
- Macro locations
- I/O locations
- Placement regions
- Power structures

---

# Good Floorplan

A good floorplan provides:

- Efficient area utilization
- Lower congestion
- Shorter interconnects
- Better timing
- Better power distribution
- Improved routability

---

# Bad Floorplan

A poor floorplan can result in:

- Routing congestion
- Long interconnects
- Timing problems
- Poor power distribution
- Area inefficiency

---

## Key Learning

Floorplanning is important because decisions made during this stage affect placement, routing, timing and power.
