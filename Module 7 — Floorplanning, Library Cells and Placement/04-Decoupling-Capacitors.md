# Decoupling Capacitors

## What is a Decoupling Capacitor?

A decoupling capacitor helps stabilize the local power supply of a circuit.

---

# Purpose

It provides local charge when there are sudden changes in current demand.

---

# Benefits

- Reduces supply voltage fluctuations
- Reduces power noise
- Improves local power stability
- Supports reliable circuit operation

---

# Placement

Decoupling capacitors are generally placed close to the circuits that require stable power.

---

## Key Learning

Decoupling capacitors help maintain a stable supply voltage and reduce the effects of sudden current variations.
